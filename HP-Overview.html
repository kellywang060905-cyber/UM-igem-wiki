UM iGEM 2026 — Human Practices

Overview

Our Compass

The threat of Listeria monocytogenes to global food safety has sounded an alarm for us. We understand that the mission of IHP extends far beyond the laboratory, it lies in needs and pain points of real world. Engagement is a two-way street—we give back to society and also learn to draw from it. Through in-depth conversations with food safety experts, consumer surveys, interviews with regulatory inspectors, and visits to food testing laboratories, we have come to understand the limitations of current L. monocytogenes detection methods and the unmet needs of those protecting our food chain. These insights have allowed us to iteratively refine our design, constantly asking and answering the pivotal question: "What makes our detection method truly better?" Along this journey, we also strive to raise public awareness and understanding of food safety, ultimately protecting the entire cold-chain storage and ensuring the safety of what we eat. This human practice journey beyond the lab not only embeds human-centered care into innovative synthetic biology, but also gives full meaning to the birth of Fridge Guardian—protection begins with listening.
